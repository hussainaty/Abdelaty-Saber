# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Curators, collaborators, students, and prospective clients looking for a concise, credible view of Abdelaty Saber’s practice. They need to understand the breadth of the work while encountering the original visual material before any promotional copy.

## Product Purpose

Present a public-facing digital archive for Abdelaty Saber, grounded only in supplied work, photographs, and verified curriculum vitae material. Success means the site makes the artist’s identity, selected disciplines, documented teaching material, and verified career milestones legible without inventing projects, results, or clients, while publishing only the contact details the user has approved.

## Brand Personality

Archival, composed, and image-led. It should feel like a mature artist’s studio inventory: quiet black surfaces, restrained gold navigation, and the authority of original Arabic visual work.

## Anti-references

Generic Behance-style card grids, invented case studies, fabricated metrics, stock imagery, decorative AI art, glassmorphism, and a junior-creative “personal brand” tone.

## Design Principles

1. Let original work lead; interface elements frame rather than compete with it.
2. Make provenance visible through concise, factual captions and clear category boundaries.
3. Treat Arabic as a primary reading mode, not a translated afterthought.
4. Use space, pacing, and a limited archive index to express depth without fictionalizing it.
5. Present only user-approved public contact information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA color contrast and keyboard access. The site supports Arabic RTL and English LTR modes, visible focus states, reduced-motion preferences, responsive reflow, descriptive image alternatives, and no auto-playing audio.
